I built a high-precision PDF-to-JSON preprocessing pipeline to clean and structure complex Chinese technical documents. I proposed a modular layout-analysis pipeline decomposing pages into text, tables, formulas, and figures, with LLM-assisted normalization for non-standard hierarchies. Implemented using RapidLayout and PaddleOCR, the system achieved state-of-the-art accuracy on Chinese tables and was adopted in an industrial LLM training pipeline.
